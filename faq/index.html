FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to the questions we hear most from business owners considering AI automation — covering scope, pricing, data safety, approvals, and what we deliberately won't build. If your question isn't here, ask us directly.

What exactly does Avokara build?

AI agents and workflow automations on the n8n platform: systems that capture and qualify leads, draft follow-ups and support replies, chase invoices, watch operations, and produce marketing content — connected to your existing tools, with human approval on important actions. See all services.

We're not technical at all. Is that a problem?

No — that's the typical client. Your team interacts through email approvals and the sheets or CRM they already use. We handle everything technical, and maintenance retainers cover ongoing care if you don't want to touch the system at all.

How is this different from hiring a Zapier freelancer?

Scope and reliability. We design the process (not just connect two apps), add AI judgement steps, build error handling and logging so failures are visible, gate risky actions behind approvals, and support the system after launch. For a simple two-app connection, a freelancer is genuinely cheaper — and we'll say so in the scoping call.

Which AI models do you use?

Commercial APIs — currently primarily OpenAI models for text and image generation, selected per task. Model choice is an implementation detail we own; systems are built so models can be swapped as better or cheaper options appear.

Is our business data safe?

Automation runs on a self-hosted n8n instance (your server or ours) with credentials encrypted at rest. Data sent to AI APIs is limited to what each task needs, and OpenAI's API terms state business data is not used to train their models. We'll walk through the exact data flow for your build in scoping.

What does it cost?

Single automations ₹15,000–₹75,000; departmental systems ₹60,000–₹1,80,000; full multi-agent systems ₹1,50,000–₹2,50,000+; maintenance from ₹12,000/month; running costs typically under ₹7,000/month. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

How long until we see results?

First automation live in 1–3 weeks; effects (faster replies, chased invoices, consistent follow-up) are visible immediately because the system starts working on real traffic from day one of going live.

Can the AI send things without our approval?

Only where you've chosen that. Default design: agent drafts, human approves with one click, then it sends. Gates are removed selectively, per workflow, when you're ready — never by default.

Do you work outside India?

Yes. We're based in India and work remotely; the systems themselves are cloud-based and location-independent. Pricing is quoted in INR by default.

What happens if we stop working with you?

You keep everything: the workflows, the documentation, and the self-hostable open-source platform they run on. No lock-in is a deliberate design choice.

Can you automate WhatsApp?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business API — approved templates, opt-out handling, compliant setup. We don't use unofficial bulk senders, which risk getting your number banned.

What won't you automate?

Payments moving without human action, legal commitments, mass cold-email spam, fake reviews or engagement, and decisions where a mistake would seriously harm a customer. Some of that is ethics, some is just good business — automated mistakes at scale are worse than manual ones.
