FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to what organisations ask before engaging us — scope, integration, security, ownership of code and data, timelines, support, procurement, and what we deliberately will not build. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Frequently asked questions

What does Avokara do?

We design, build, integrate and maintain software: custom web applications and portals, AI-driven automation, API integrations between existing systems, operational dashboards and MIS, and the infrastructure they run on. A project is taken from requirement discovery through architecture, development, integration, testing, deployment and training, and supported afterwards. See services.

What kind of organisations do you work with?

Organisations with a workflow complex enough that software has to be built around it: institutions and public-service bodies, educational and healthcare organisations, financial and professional services firms, enterprises, manufacturers and distributors, and growing businesses. The common factor is multiple roles, approvals, records and reporting obligations — not a particular headcount.

Do you only build AI systems?

No. Most of what we build is ordinary, well-engineered software: data models, workflows, interfaces, integrations and reporting. AI is used where work genuinely requires reading and interpreting something. A rule that must always hold is written as code, because a model that is right most of the time is not a control.

Can you integrate with systems we already run?

Yes, and it is a large part of our work. We design and consume REST APIs, build middleware and scheduled synchronisation, and integrate identity, payment, messaging and third-party platforms. Where a system has no API we work with the interfaces it does expose and are explicit about the limits of that. See API and system integration.

Can the system run on our own servers?

Yes. Deployment is containerised, so the same tested build runs on your hardware, in your cloud account, or on infrastructure we manage. Where data must remain inside your environment, that is a design input from the first stage rather than a later change.

How do you handle security and access control?

As part of architecture, not a review at the end: role-based access control with least-privilege defaults, authentication and session handling, server-side input validation, parameterised queries, encrypted secrets, TLS in transit, audit logging of privileged actions, environment separation and tested backups. Full detail on the security and reliability page.

Do you hold ISO 27001, SOC 2 or any security empanelment?

No. Avokara does not currently hold third-party information-security certifications, and does not hold or claim any security empanelment, accreditation or government clearance. We state this plainly because discovering it later wastes your time. Where an engagement requires independent penetration testing or a compliance attestation, we say so during scoping and work with an accredited third party; systems are built and documented so such a review is possible.

Who owns the source code and the data?

You do. Source is delivered in a version-control repository you control, with architecture and administration documentation. Data is yours throughout and exportable in standard formats. We build on standard open technologies specifically so the work does not depend on us continuing.

How long does a project take?

Scope-dependent, and quoted per stage rather than as a single number. As a guide: a single workflow or integration is typically weeks; a departmental application with several roles and integrations is typically a few months; a programme across functions is sequenced into releases so something useful reaches production early. The timeline is committed after discovery, not before.

What do you need from us during a project?

A named person who can answer process questions and make decisions; access to the systems being integrated; and availability from the people who will actually use the system for prototype review and testing. Projects slow down over decision availability far more often than over engineering.

Do you support and maintain systems long term?

Yes, and it is scoped as part of the original engagement rather than raised after go-live, so the cost of owning the system is known before it is built. Monitoring, fixes, security and dependency updates, performance work and an allowance for small changes, under written response expectations. See support and maintenance.

Can you take over a system someone else built?

Often, after an assessment of the code, data model, deployment and documentation. You get a written finding first: what is supportable, what needs remediation, and what the risks are. Where the honest answer is that it should be rebuilt, we will say so rather than accept a maintenance fee for something we cannot stand behind.

What does it cost?

Fixed price against a fixed written scope, quoted after requirement discovery. Indicative ranges for common scopes and the factors that move a project within them are on the engagement models page. Programme-scale work is quoted individually rather than estimated on a web page.

Will you work to our procurement, contract and documentation process?

Yes — your agreement template, purchase-order process, milestone certification, invoicing requirements, NDA, security questionnaire or vendor-onboarding documentation. Company registration documents are available on request, and the capability profile is printable as a PDF for an evaluation file.

Do you work outside your own state, or outside India?

We are based in India and work with clients across the country, on site where an engagement needs it and remotely otherwise. The systems themselves are location-independent. Pricing is quoted in INR by default.

What will you not build?

Unattended movement of money or legal commitment without human confirmation. Mass unsolicited messaging, or messaging through unofficial channels that puts your number or domain at risk. Systems designed to fabricate reviews, engagement or records. Access to a third-party system that has not been legitimately authorised. And anything where an automated error would seriously harm an individual without a person in the loop.

How experienced is the company?

Avokara Private Limited was incorporated in June 2026, and we would rather tell you that than have you find it during evaluation. What we offer instead of a long client list is verifiable technical substance: the internal case study documents eleven production workflows running our own operations, with the architecture, the running cost and the four things that went wrong. Assess the engineering on that.

Question not answered here?

Ask it directly. If the answer is that we are not the right company for your requirement, you will get that answer too.
