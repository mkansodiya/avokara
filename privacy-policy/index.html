Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

Avokara Private Limited ("Avokara") collects only the information you submit through our contact form or email — name, email address, company, and your message — and uses it solely to respond to your enquiry and deliver services. We do not sell personal data, run advertising trackers, or share data with third parties except the service providers needed to operate (listed below).

Information we collect

  • Contact information you submit via the form or email: name, email, company, message content.
  • Basic technical data collected by our hosting provider (such as IP addresses in server logs) for security and reliability.

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule calls.
  • To deliver and support contracted services.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Service providers

We use a small number of processors to operate: website hosting (GitHub Pages), form handling (Formspree), and email (Google Workspace/Gmail). During service delivery, client business data processed by automations may pass through AI API providers (such as OpenAI) strictly as required by the contracted workflows; per OpenAI's API terms, such data is not used to train their models.

Retention and your rights

Enquiry data is kept as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting engagement. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to hello@avokaraai.com; we respond within 30 days.

Privacy questions: hello@avokaraai.com — Avokara Private Limited, India.
