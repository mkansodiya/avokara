What Does Business Automation Cost in India? Honest Numbers

By Nitin Singh · 2026-08-01 · 6 min read · Last updated 2026-08-02

Direct answer: For a small or mid-sized business in India, expect roughly ₹15,000–₹75,000 for a single well-built automation, ₹60,000–₹2,50,000 for a multi-workflow business system, ₹500–₹5,000/month in AI and platform running costs, and ₹12,000–₹60,000/month if you want ongoing maintenance and tuning. Below is where each number comes from.

The four cost layers

1. Platform costs

Managed platforms (Zapier, Make) charge per task — from free tiers to ₹2,000–₹15,000+/month as volume grows. Self-hosted n8n costs only its server: ₹400–₹1,500/month for a VPS that handles typical SMB volumes. This is why volume forecasting matters before choosing a platform — see our platform comparison.

2. AI usage costs

LLM APIs are priced per token (roughly, per word processed). Real-world SMB examples: qualifying and drafting replies for a few hundred leads a month costs a few hundred rupees; generating three high-quality branded images a week costs roughly ₹250–₹350/month at current OpenAI image pricing. AI usage is rarely the significant cost at SMB scale — but it should always be estimated upfront, and we do.

3. Build costs

The real cost driver. What you're paying for is process design, integration work, prompt engineering, error handling, and testing against real traffic. Honest ranges from our own quoting: a single workflow (invoice reminders, one follow-up sequence) ₹15,000–₹75,000; a departmental system (full sales pipeline automation) ₹60,000–₹1,80,000; a multi-department system with AI agents and approval gates ₹1,50,000–₹2,50,000+.

4. Maintenance

APIs change, edge cases appear, prompts drift. Options: pay-per-fix (fine for simple systems), a retainer of ₹12,000–₹60,000/month depending on system size (typical for AI-heavy systems), or in-house handover with documentation. Any vendor who doesn't raise maintenance is deferring the conversation to a worse time.

What makes the price go up

  • Number of systems touched: each CRM, inbox, or accounting tool adds integration and testing work.
  • Judgement depth: a notification is cheap; a decision with edge cases, approvals, and money involved costs more to get right.
  • Data quality: messy or undocumented data adds cleanup work before automation is safe.
  • Compliance surface: WhatsApp Business API setup, e-mail deliverability, and audit logging add real work when required.

What a fair quote looks like

A quote you can trust should itemise: what is being built (workflow by workflow), what it connects to, where the human approval points are, expected monthly running costs, what maintenance costs after handover, and what is explicitly out of scope. Fixed-price after a scoping conversation beats hourly billing for well-defined automation work — it puts the estimation risk on the builder, where it belongs. That's how our engagement models are structured.

Related questions

Why not just hire a Zapier freelancer for ₹5,000?

For one simple zap, that can be exactly right. The gap appears in error handling, edge cases, and maintenance — cheap builds tend to fail silently and cost more to rescue later. Match the investment to how much the process matters.

Are there hidden costs to watch for?

The common ones: per-task platform fees growing with your volume, WhatsApp Business API conversation charges, and vendor lock-in where you can't maintain what was built. Ask any vendor these three questions directly.

Is automation worth it for a very small business?

Below a certain volume, discipline beats automation — a checklist and calendar reminders are free. The crossover comes when repetitive volume regularly eats hours weekly or leads/invoices start slipping through cracks. We'll tell you honestly in a scoping call if you're below that line.
