n8n vs Zapier vs Make: Which Automation Platform for a Small Business?

By Nitin Singh · 2026-07-27 · 8 min read · Last updated 2026-08-02

Direct answer: Choose Zapier if you need a handful of simple, low-volume connections and want zero maintenance. Choose Make for more complex visual workflows at mid volumes on a budget. Choose n8n if you want AI-heavy workflows, high volumes without per-task fees, or your data kept on your own server — and you have (or hire) someone to maintain it. We build on n8n at Avokara, but Zapier is genuinely the right answer for some businesses.

Where each one wins

Zapier: simplicity

Fastest setup, biggest template library, least maintenance. The trade-off is cost at volume — per-task pricing means a busy workflow can cost more monthly than a server that could run hundreds of workflows. If you need three simple connections and money isn't tight, Zapier is fine and we'll tell you so.

Make: visual power at mid price

Make's scenario editor handles branching and iteration well, and its per-operation pricing is friendlier than Zapier's at moderate volume. It sits between the other two in most dimensions.

n8n: control, AI, and economics at scale

n8n is open source and self-hostable: a ₹400–₹1,500/month VPS runs unlimited workflows and executions. Its AI capabilities are the strongest of the three — native LLM nodes, agent patterns, and full JavaScript when you need it. The honest cost: someone must maintain the server and updates. That's the trade Avokara absorbs for clients — we build and maintain n8n systems so businesses get the economics without the ops burden.

The volume crossover

The decision often reduces to volume. At tens of tasks a month, managed platforms are cheaper than any server. At thousands of tasks a month — every lead, order, and ticket flowing through automation — per-task pricing inverts, and self-hosted n8n typically becomes the cheapest option by a wide margin. Run your expected monthly task count against current Zapier/Make pricing before choosing; the answer usually becomes obvious.

Related questions

Is n8n really free?

The self-hosted community edition is free and open source; you pay only for the server it runs on. n8n also sells cloud hosting and enterprise features. Most SMB use cases are fully covered by the free self-hosted edition.

Can I migrate from Zapier to n8n later?

Yes — workflows must be rebuilt rather than imported, but the logic maps over directly. Many of our n8n projects started as Zapier cost-reduction migrations.

Which is best for AI agents?

n8n, clearly, as of 2026: native LLM nodes, agent tooling, memory patterns, and custom code make it the practical choice for AI-heavy automation among the three.
