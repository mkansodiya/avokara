Discuss a technology requirement

Describe the requirement and the workflow behind it. We reply within one business day to arrange a requirement discussion, and you will get an honest view of what we would build, how we would architect it and what we would not recommend — whether or not you engage us.

What happens next

  1. You describe the requirement. A few lines is enough to start: what the process is, where it stalls, and which systems are involved.
  2. We reply within one business day to arrange a requirement discussion, and tell you what information would be useful to have ready.
  3. Requirement discussion. We work through objectives, users, the workflow as actually performed, existing systems and constraints.
  4. Written response. Proposed scope, architecture approach, stage plan, timeline, commercials, and what is explicitly out of scope. No obligation either way.
