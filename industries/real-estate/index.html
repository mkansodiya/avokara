AI automation for Real Estate

Real-estate sales cycles are long and enquiry response speed decides who gets the client. The automations that matter: replying to every portal or ad enquiry within minutes with relevant listings, nurturing leads over weeks with useful updates rather than nagging, scheduling and reminding for site visits, and turning new listings into ready-to-post content.

Problems we see most often

  • Portal enquiries (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing) answered hours or days late
  • Leads who aren't ready this month getting forgotten instead of nurtured
  • Site visits booked over calls and then missed for lack of reminders
  • Every new listing needing the same manual round of posts and messages

Automations that pay back fastest

Instant enquiry response

Every enquiry gets a same-day drafted reply with matching listings and a call-to-action to book a visit.

Long-cycle nurturing

Monthly useful touchpoints — new matching listings, price updates — until the lead is ready, with all history logged.

Visit scheduling and reminders

Confirmations and day-before reminders by email or WhatsApp reduce no-shows.

Listing content pipeline

New listing details turned into portal descriptions and social posts with branded graphics.

Tools we commonly connect

Gmail, WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets, portal enquiry emails, Meta lead ads, Google Calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Our leads come from many portals — can they all feed one system?

Yes. Portal enquiry emails, Meta lead-ad forms, and website forms all flow into one pipeline with source tracking, so you see which channel produces buyers, not just enquiries.

Property matching is judgement — can AI do it?

AI drafts a shortlist from your inventory based on stated budget, location, and type; the broker reviews before it goes out. It's a fast first pass, not a replacement for your market knowledge.

Is WhatsApp automation allowed?

Yes, via the official WhatsApp Business API with approved message templates and opt-out handling. We set this up compliantly — no grey-market bulk senders, which risk your number being banned.
