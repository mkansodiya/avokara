AI automation for E-commerce & Retail

For e-commerce businesses, the highest-value automations are usually customer-facing repetition: answering “where is my order” from live order data, following up on enquiries and abandoned conversations, requesting reviews after delivery, and alerting you to low stock or stuck orders. These are high-volume, rule-friendly tasks where automation pays back fastest.

Problems we see most often

  • Support inbox dominated by order-status and return-policy questions
  • Enquiries from marketplace chats and Instagram DMs that go cold before anyone replies
  • Review requests sent inconsistently, or not at all
  • Stock-outs discovered only when a customer orders something you don't have

Automations that pay back fastest

Order-status auto-replies

Support agent reads the query, looks up the order in Shopify/WooCommerce or your sheet, and drafts an accurate reply — most e-commerce ticket volume handled in minutes.

Enquiry follow-up

Every product enquiry gets a same-day personalised reply and a scheduled follow-up if it goes quiet.

Post-delivery review requests

Timed, polite review asks after confirmed delivery, spaced to avoid spamming.

Inventory and anomaly alerts

Daily digest flags low stock, unusual order patterns, and stuck fulfilments.

Tools we commonly connect

Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon/Flipkart seller reports, Razorpay, Shiprocket, Gmail, WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets.

Frequently asked questions

Can it answer order-status questions accurately?

Yes — the reply is drafted from your live order data (Shopify, WooCommerce, or your fulfilment sheet), not guessed by the AI. If the order can't be found, the query escalates to a human instead of guessing.

Does it work with WhatsApp, where most of my customers are?

Yes, through the WhatsApp Business API. Outbound messages use approved templates; inbound conversations can be triaged and drafted like email.

What does a typical e-commerce build cost?

A support + follow-up automation package typically lands between ₹60,000 and ₹1,50,000 depending on order volume and channels, plus modest monthly AI usage costs. See pricing for engagement models.
