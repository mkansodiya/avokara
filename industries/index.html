Sectors we work in

The engineering is common across sectors. What differs is the workflow, the terminology, the reporting obligations and who has to approve what. These are the sectors where the problems we solve occur most often.

Avokara builds software, integrations and automation for organisations across public services and institutions, healthcare, education, financial services, agriculture and rural programmes, manufacturing and logistics, retail and commerce, real estate and infrastructure, and professional services and NGOs. Sector experience matters less than it is usually claimed to; understanding the specific workflow matters more, which is why every engagement starts with requirement discovery.

A note on sector experience

What actually transfers between sectors

Suppliers tend to oversell sector specialisation. In our experience the things that genuinely transfer are structural, and they transfer almost completely:

Solution areas in detail

  • Approval chains behave the same whether the record is a licence application, a purchase requisition, a patient referral or an admission form.
  • Role-scoped access is the same problem whether the unit is a district, a branch, a department or a campus.
  • Queues and ageing are the same problem whether the item is a case, a ticket, a consignment or an invoice.
  • Document verification is the same problem whether the document is a certificate, an invoice, a report or a proof of identity.
  • Consolidated reporting is the same problem whether the dimension is region, branch, product or programme.

What does not transfer is the specific workflow, the terminology and the regulatory obligations — which is exactly why requirement discovery is stage one rather than something we claim to already know.
