About

About Avokara

Avokara Private Limited is an Indian technology company that designs, builds, integrates and maintains software: custom web applications and portals, AI-driven automation, API integrations between existing systems, operational dashboards and MIS, and the infrastructure they run on. Incorporated in June 2026 and founded by Nitin Singh, it works with institutions, enterprises and growing organisations across India.

What we actually do

We are engaged when a workflow is specific enough that software has to be built around it rather than bought off a shelf; when systems that need to exchange data do not; when a process still runs on paper, registers or spreadsheets; or when the monthly report is assembled by hand from four places.

The work covers the whole lifecycle, because dividing it is where most projects lose time: requirement discovery, architecture, interface design, development, integration, testing, deployment, documentation, training, and support afterwards. One team carries it across all of those. See engineering capabilities for what that covers technically and delivery process for how a project runs.

How we work

  • Requirement before quote. We do not price a system before understanding the process it has to support. Discovery is a stage with an output — a scope document, a process map, and a written list of assumptions and exclusions — not a sales call.
  • The process as performed, not as documented. Almost every process has informal workarounds, and that is usually where the real requirement is. A system built from the official description alone gets rejected by the people who have to use it.
  • AI where it earns its place. Language models are used for work that genuinely needs reading and interpretation. Rules that must always hold are written as code, because a model that is right most of the time is not a control.
  • Human confirmation by default. Consequential actions are drafted by the system and confirmed by a named person. Controls are removed deliberately, per workflow, once you are confident — not assumed away at the start.
  • Built to be handed over. Standard open technologies, source code in a repository you own, written architecture and administration documentation. If you take the work in-house or move it to another vendor, nothing has to be reverse-engineered.
  • An honest no. If an off-the-shelf product covers your requirement, if a process is too low-volume to justify a system, or if something is outside what we can deliver well, we say so. It costs us a project occasionally and saves a great deal of mutual time.

Where we are, honestly

Avokara Private Limited was incorporated in June 2026. It is a young company, and we would rather you know that from us than discover it during evaluation. What we publish instead of a client list is verifiable technical substance: the architecture, running cost and failure modes of systems we have actually built — see the internal case study, which documents eleven production workflows running our own operations, including the four things that went wrong.

We do not publish invented client results, anonymised composites, statistics we cannot show you, or certifications we do not hold. Client projects appear as case studies when they complete and the client agrees to publication, with the same level of technical detail.

The founder

Nitin Singh founded Avokara and leads requirement discovery, solution architecture and delivery. Every requirement discussion is with the person who will design the system, not a sales intermediary — which is the practical advantage of working with a small, technically-led company, and the reason we are selective about how many engagements run at once.
