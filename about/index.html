About

About Avokara

Avokara Private Limited is an AI business-automation agency founded by Nitin Singh, based in India and working with clients remotely. We build AI agents and n8n workflow systems for small and mid-sized businesses — practical automation with human approval gates, fixed-price projects, and honest advice about what not to automate.

Why we exist

Most small businesses lose money to the same quiet leaks: enquiries answered late, proposals never chased, invoices reminded about awkwardly or not at all, support inboxes that eat entire days. None of this is a strategy problem — it's a repetition problem. Large companies hire operations teams to absorb it. Small businesses can't, and until recently, software that could handle judgement-adjacent work didn't exist at small-business prices.

That changed with large language models. An AI agent can now read an enquiry, understand it, draft a good reply, and know when to ask a human. Avokara exists to turn that capability into reliable, boring, production systems for businesses that don't have — and shouldn't need — an engineering team.

How we work

  • Human approval by default. Anything customer-facing or financial is drafted by the agent and approved by a person, until you decide otherwise.
  • Fixed quotes, honest scoping. We quote after a free scoping call, workflow by workflow, with running costs estimated upfront. If a ₹5,000 Zapier zap solves your problem, we'll say so.
  • Built on open source. We build on n8n, which you can self-host — your data stays on your server, and there are no per-task fees at scale.
  • We use what we sell. Avokara's own operations — lead handling, approvals, daily digests, invoice reminders, LinkedIn content — run on the same system we build for clients. Read the internal case study.

The founder

Nitin Singh founded Avokara to bring AI automation to the businesses that benefit from it most — small and mid-sized companies where every saved hour is felt directly. He leads every scoping call and system design personally.
