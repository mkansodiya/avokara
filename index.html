How it works

From scoping call to live system

  1. Free scoping call (30 min). We map your processes and tell you honestly which are worth automating — and which aren't.
  2. Fixed quote. Workflow-by-workflow scope, timeline, running costs, and what's explicitly excluded. No hourly billing surprises.
  3. Build & test (1–6 weeks). We build on n8n with your tools, test against real traffic, and put approval gates where they belong.
  4. Go live with training. Your team learns the approve-and-monitor routine — usually minutes a day.
  5. Tune & maintain. 30 days of included tuning, then optional maintenance or a full handover with documentation.

Common questions

What does Avokara actually do?

We design, build, and maintain AI agents and workflow automations for small and mid-sized businesses — systems that qualify leads, follow up, answer support queries, chase invoices, and generate marketing content, with a human approving anything important. See services for the full list.

Who is this for?

Small and mid-sized businesses — typically 2 to 200 people — that run on tools like Gmail, Google Sheets, WhatsApp, Shopify, or a CRM, and are losing time or leads to repetitive manual work. We work with clients across India and remotely.

How long does a project take?

A single automation is typically live in 1–3 weeks; a multi-workflow business system takes 2–6 weeks. Every project starts with a free scoping call and a fixed quote.

Do you use our existing tools or force new software?

We build on what you already use. The automation platform (n8n) works alongside your current tools via their APIs — your team keeps working in the inbox, sheet, or CRM they know.
