Finance & Operations Automation

Finance and operations automation handles the repetitive back-office work that eats owner time: chasing unpaid invoices on a polite escalating schedule, tracking what's been paid, flagging stuck work across the business, and sending a single daily digest of everything that needs attention. Avokara builds these workflows with approval gates on any outgoing money-related communication.

Book a Free Scoping Call

What's included

  • Invoice reminder sequences: polite reminder at 7 days overdue, firmer at 15, escalation to you at 30 — each approved before sending
  • Payment status tracking written back to your invoice sheet or accounting tool
  • Daily operations digest: one email each morning listing stuck leads, overdue invoices, and unanswered tickets
  • Stuck-work detection across your pipeline with configurable thresholds
  • Activity log of every automated action for auditability

Timeline

1–3 weeks. Invoice reminders alone can be live within a week.

Pricing

Typical builds range from ₹40,000 to ₹1,20,000. Often combined with other automations in a larger package. See engagement models for how projects and retainers are structured.

Typical use cases

Receivables chasing

Overdue invoices get followed up on a fixed, polite schedule without you having to remember or have the awkward conversation.

Owner's daily digest

One morning email replaces checking five different tools to see what needs attention.

Approval workflows

Anything above a threshold — a discount, a refund, an unusual payment — routes to the owner for one-click approval by email.

Honest limitations

  • We automate communication and tracking around money, not the movement of money itself — payments always require your explicit action in your banking or payment system.
  • Integration depth depends on your accounting tool's API. Tally requires a connector setup; Zoho Books and QuickBooks integrate directly.

Frequently asked questions

Will automated reminders annoy my clients?

The schedule and tone are yours to set, and every reminder can go through your approval first. Most businesses find a polite, consistent schedule gets invoices paid faster and creates less friction than sporadic manual chasing.

Can it connect to Tally?

Yes, via Tally's XML/HTTP interface or an export step, though it takes more setup than cloud tools like Zoho Books. We scope this precisely in the free call.

What's in the daily digest?

Whatever you choose. A typical digest lists: new leads and their status, invoices overdue with amounts, tickets unanswered past your SLA, and anything the automation flagged as stuck or unusual.
