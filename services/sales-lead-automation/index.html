Sales & Lead Automation

Sales and lead automation means every enquiry from your website, ads, or marketplace gets captured into one pipeline, qualified by AI against your ideal-customer profile, and followed up on schedule — first reply, reminders, and proposal tracking — without a salesperson having to remember. Avokara builds this on n8n with your email and CRM, with approval gates so a human signs off before anything is sent.

Discuss a Requirement

What's included

  • Lead capture from web forms, email enquiries, and ad platforms into one pipeline (Google Sheets or your CRM)
  • AI qualification and scoring against your ideal-customer profile, with a short research summary per lead
  • Personalised first-response drafts, approved by you before sending
  • Automated follow-up sequences with write-backs, so the same lead is never emailed twice by mistake
  • Proposal-sent and reply tracking with reminders for the sales owner
  • Weekly pipeline summary email

Timeline

2–4 weeks to go live. Week 1 is process mapping and pipeline setup, weeks 2–3 are build and testing with real leads, week 4 is tuning.

Pricing

Typical builds range from ₹60,000 to ₹1,80,000 depending on lead volume and how many sources feed the pipeline. Maintenance from ₹15,000/month. See engagement models for how projects and retainers are structured.

Typical use cases

Speed-to-lead

New enquiry gets a personalised, approved reply the same day instead of days later — response speed is one of the biggest controllable factors in lead conversion.

Follow-up discipline

Leads that go quiet get scheduled follow-ups at day 3, 7, and 14 automatically, with status written back so nothing repeats.

Proposal chasing

When a proposal goes out, the system tracks the reply and reminds the owner to chase at the right time.

Honest limitations

  • Automation improves speed and consistency; it does not replace a human closing conversation for high-value deals.
  • Cold outreach at scale is deliberately out of scope — spam damages your domain reputation. We automate response to inbound interest and warm follow-up.
  • If your leads arrive by phone call only, we first need a step that captures them digitally (a form, a WhatsApp flow, or a quick CRM entry).

Frequently asked questions

How fast will leads get a first reply?

As fast as your approval. The agent drafts the reply within minutes of the enquiry arriving; once someone on your team approves it, it sends immediately. Teams that check approvals twice a day typically get first replies out the same day.

Will it work with my existing CRM?

Yes, if the CRM has an API — Zoho, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Freshsales are common. If you don't have a CRM, we set up a structured Google Sheet pipeline that can migrate to a CRM later.

Can it handle WhatsApp leads?

Yes, via the WhatsApp Business API. Note that WhatsApp requires an approved business account and template messages for outbound; we help set that up.

What does it cost to run monthly?

The AI usage cost for a typical typical lead volume (a few hundred leads a month) is usually under ₹2,000/month. Hosting the automation platform is free if self-hosted or from about ₹1,700/month on n8n's cloud plans.
