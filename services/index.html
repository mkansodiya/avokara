Questions about services

Which service do we need?

Most requirements need a combination, which is why we scope from the problem rather than the service list. A new departmental system is typically custom software plus integration plus reporting plus support; digitising an existing paper process is digital transformation plus custom software. Describe the workflow and we will tell you what it actually needs — including where the answer is less work than you expected.

Can you take on just one part of a larger project?

Yes. A defined technical scope — an API layer, an integration, a dashboard, a data migration, a technical review — delivered alongside your existing team or vendor is a normal engagement for us.

Do you work on a fixed price or time basis?

Fixed scope and fixed price after requirement discovery is the usual model for a build, because it gives both sides something to hold. Large scopes are sequenced into releases, each quoted individually. Ongoing support is a monthly arrangement. See engagement models.

What if our requirement is not on this list?

Ask. The list describes what we are most often engaged for, not the boundary of what we can build. If a requirement is outside what we can deliver well, we will say so plainly rather than take the work and learn at your expense.

Have a requirement to scope?

Send the requirement, the workflow behind it, or a scope document you are working to. We will respond with what we would build and what we would not.
