n8n Workflow Automation

n8n is an open-source workflow automation platform — an alternative to Zapier and Make — that can be self-hosted, connects to 400+ tools, and has strong built-in support for AI steps. Avokara designs and builds n8n workflows, sets up secure self-hosted or cloud instances, and maintains them. Self-hosting means no per-task fees: heavy automation runs at server cost only.

  • Workflow design and build for any process across your tools
  • Self-hosted n8n setup (Docker on your VPS or ours) or n8n Cloud configuration
  • AI-step integration: LLM calls, image generation, and document processing inside workflows
  • Error-handler and alerting patterns so failures are visible, never silent
  • Documentation and handover, or ongoing maintenance — your choice

Honest limitations

  • Self-hosting requires a server (a ₹400–₹1,500/month VPS is typical) and someone responsible for updates — that can be us under maintenance, but it's a real responsibility.
  • n8n is the right tool for server-side workflow automation; it is not a replacement for RPA that clicks through desktop applications without APIs.

Frequently asked questions

Why n8n instead of Zapier or Make?

Three reasons: self-hosting removes per-task fees (heavy workflows cost the same as light ones), your data stays on your own server, and n8n's AI nodes make LLM-powered steps first-class. Zapier remains a fine choice for very simple, low-volume connections — we'll tell you honestly if that's all you need. See our detailed n8n vs Zapier vs Make comparison.

Where is the data hosted?

Your choice: on a VPS you own (we set it up), on our managed infrastructure, or on n8n's official cloud. For self-hosted setups, credentials are encrypted at rest and workflows run entirely on your server.

What happens if a workflow breaks?

Every system we ship includes an error-handler workflow: failures send an immediate alert with details, and the activity log shows exactly what ran. Under maintenance retainers we fix breakages as part of the service.
