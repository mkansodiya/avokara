Customer Support Automation

Support automation uses AI to read every incoming ticket or support email, classify it by topic and urgency, draft a reply for routine questions using your own knowledge base and past answers, and route complex or sensitive cases straight to a person. The result is faster first responses on the 60–80% of tickets that are routine, and more human time for the cases that need it.

Discuss a Requirement

What's included

  • Support inbox triage: classification by topic, urgency, and sentiment
  • AI-drafted replies for routine questions, grounded in your documented policies and past answers
  • Escalation rules: refunds, complaints, and anything the AI is unsure about goes to a human immediately
  • Ticket log with status tracking, so nothing sits unanswered past your SLA
  • Daily summary of ticket volume, topics, and anything overdue

Timeline

2–4 weeks. The first week is spent studying your real ticket history to design categories and safe reply templates.

Pricing

Typical builds range from ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000. Maintenance from ₹12,000/month. See engagement models for how projects and retainers are structured.

Typical use cases

Order status and logistics queries

The largest single category for e-commerce — answered instantly from your order data.

Policy questions

Returns, warranties, and service terms answered consistently from your documented policies.

After-hours cover

Routine queries get a useful, accurate holding reply outside business hours, with morning escalation for the rest.

Honest limitations

  • The AI only answers from your documented knowledge — if your policies are undocumented or inconsistent, we document them first as part of the build.
  • Angry customers, refund approvals, and legal threats are always routed to humans. Automating those is bad business, and we won't.
  • Voice/phone support automation is out of scope for standard builds; we focus on email, chat, and WhatsApp text support.

Frequently asked questions

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

That's your choice, and we recommend transparency. A common setup: AI drafts, a human approves and sends from the normal support address. For fully automated replies we recommend a clear signature line and an easy way to reach a human.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

Replies are grounded in your knowledge base rather than the model's general knowledge, uncertain cases are escalated instead of guessed, and during the pilot phase every reply goes through human approval so we can measure accuracy on your real traffic before loosening the gate.

Can it work with Freshdesk / Zendesk / a shared Gmail inbox?

Yes. We commonly build on a shared Gmail or Google Workspace inbox for smaller teams, and integrate with Freshdesk, Zendesk, or Zoho Desk via API for teams already using them.
