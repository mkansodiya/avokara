Marketing & Content Automation

Marketing automation at Avokara means a content pipeline that runs on schedule: it researches current news and trends in your industry, writes a post in your voice with a genuine point of view, generates a branded graphic to match, and sends the ready-to-publish package for your approval. You stay the author; the machine does the assembly. We run this system for our own LinkedIn presence.

Book a Free Scoping Call

What's included

  • Trend research from news feeds and sources relevant to your industry
  • Post drafts written in a defined voice — hooks, concrete details, and a question to drive comments, never generic filler
  • Branded graphics generated per post with your logo and brand colours applied automatically
  • Ready-to-post email or scheduled publishing, your choice
  • Content calendar tracking of everything drafted, approved, and published

Timeline

1–2 weeks to set up the pipeline, then it runs on your chosen schedule (for example three posts a week).

Pricing

Setup typically ₹40,000–₹90,000, then running costs of roughly ₹500–₹2,000/month in AI usage depending on volume and image quality. See engagement models for how projects and retainers are structured.

Typical use cases

Founder-brand LinkedIn presence

Consistent, opinionated posts tied to real industry news — the system we run for Avokara itself.

Newsletter assembly

Weekly customer newsletter drafted from your updates, offers, and industry news, ready for a 10-minute review.

Product content

New products or offers turned into announcement posts and graphics automatically.

Honest limitations

  • AI drafts need a human editor for voice and judgement — we design the workflow assuming you'll spend a few minutes per post, not zero.
  • Fully automated posting to LinkedIn requires a verified LinkedIn company page and API access; until then the pipeline delivers ready-to-post packages by email.
  • We do not fabricate statistics, fake engagement, or astroturf comments. The system's job is making your real expertise consistent, not faking one.

Frequently asked questions

Will the posts sound like AI?

The writing prompt is built from your actual voice: sentence length, opinions, vocabulary, and topics you care about. Posts reference one specific current story with a concrete detail rather than summarising generic news. And you approve every post, so nothing that sounds off goes out.

Who owns the content?

You do, entirely — the drafts, the graphics, and the pipeline configuration.

Can it post automatically without me?

Technically yes once platform API access is set up, but we recommend keeping a quick human approval on brand-voice content. Most clients approve from their phone in under a minute.
