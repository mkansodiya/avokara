Pricing

Engagement models and honest price ranges

Avokara works on fixed-price projects quoted after a free scoping call, with optional monthly maintenance. Indicative ranges: single automations ₹15,000–₹75,000; departmental systems ₹60,000–₹1,80,000; multi-agent business systems ₹1,50,000–₹2,50,000+; maintenance retainers ₹12,000–₹60,000/month. Running costs (AI usage + hosting) are estimated upfront and typically total under ₹7,000/month for SMB volumes.

Maintenance retainers

After the included 30-day tuning period: ₹12,000–₹60,000/month depending on system size, covering monitoring, breakage fixes, prompt tuning, and small changes. Alternatively, take the documented handover and self-manage — the platform is open source, and you're never locked in.

What affects where you land in the range

  • Number of tools integrated (each API adds build and test work)
  • Depth of AI judgement required (notification vs decision with edge cases)
  • Data readiness (clean sheets vs cleanup-first)
  • Compliance surface (WhatsApp Business API, deliverability, audit needs)

Pricing questions

Why ranges instead of exact prices?

Because scope varies: a lead-followup build touching one inbox and one sheet costs less than one integrating a CRM, WhatsApp, and three ad platforms. The range tells you honestly what to expect; the fixed quote after scoping tells you exactly.

What are the ongoing costs after the build?

Three possible lines: AI API usage (typically ₹500–₹5,000/month at SMB volumes — estimated for your case upfront), hosting (₹0 on your existing server, or ₹400–₹1,700/month for a VPS or n8n cloud), and optional maintenance retainer if you want us to keep tuning and fixing.

Do you charge for the scoping call?

No. The 30-minute scoping call is free and carries no obligation. You leave with an honest read on what's worth automating, even if you build it with someone else.

What if the automation doesn't work as promised?

Every build includes 30 days of post-launch tuning against real traffic, and quotes itemise exactly what the system will do — so 'working' is defined in writing before we start, not argued about after.
