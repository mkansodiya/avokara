2. What Avokara does

Avokara designs, builds, integrates and maintains software. That covers custom web applications and portals, AI-driven automation, API development and integration between existing systems, operational dashboards and MIS, and the cloud infrastructure these run on. A project is taken from requirement discovery through architecture, development, integration, testing, deployment and training, and supported afterwards.

We are engaged where a workflow is too specific to fit off-the-shelf software, where systems that need to exchange data do not, where a process still runs on paper or spreadsheets, or where reporting is assembled by hand.

5. Solution areas

  • Process Digitisation — A process that currently lives in registers, printed forms or a shared spreadsheet becomes a system: defined roles, defined states, a record of who did what and when, and data you can report on.
  • Workflow Automation — Repetitive coordination — routing, reminders, escalations, status updates, handovers — is executed by software on a schedule or a trigger, instead of people chasing each other.
  • Digital Service Platforms — A portal through which an organisation delivers a service to the people who use it: they submit, they track, they receive; your team processes the queue in a structured back office.
  • Monitoring, MIS & Reporting — The numbers a decision needs are in one place and refresh themselves, instead of being assembled from several systems each time someone asks.
  • Document & Data Automation — Information is extracted, classified, validated and routed out of documents and records at volume, with low-confidence cases sent to a person rather than guessed at.
  • Communication Automation — Structured, template-based communication over web, email, WhatsApp and SMS — sent on rules, logged, and with opt-out and consent handling built in.
  • System Integration — Existing systems, databases and third-party platforms are connected so that data is entered once and moves reliably, with failures visible rather than silent.

8. Security and reliability

Security and access control are addressed during architecture and implemented throughout the application lifecycle: role-based access control with least-privilege defaults, server-side input validation, parameterised queries, encrypted secrets, TLS in transit, audit logging of privileged actions, environment separation, scheduled backups with tested restores, monitoring with alerting, and human confirmation before consequential automated actions. Full detail is on the security and reliability page.

Certifications. Avokara does not currently hold third-party information-security certifications such as ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2, and does not hold or claim any security empanelment, accreditation or government security clearance. Where an engagement requires independent penetration testing, a security audit or a compliance attestation, we state that during scoping and work with an accredited third party; systems are documented so that such a review is possible.

10. Engagement models

  • Fixed-scope project. Defined scope, deliverables, timeline and payment stages, quoted in writing after requirement discovery. The usual model for a build.
  • Staged programme. A large scope sequenced into releases, each with its own scope and sign-off, so a working part reaches production early.
  • Support and maintenance. Monitoring, fixes, security and dependency updates, performance work and an agreed enhancement allowance after go-live.
  • Defined technical scope alongside your team. An API layer, an integration, a dashboard, a migration or a review, delivered alongside your existing team or vendor.

Indicative commercial ranges and what moves a project within them are on the engagement models page. Every engagement is quoted in writing before work begins; GST additional where applicable.
